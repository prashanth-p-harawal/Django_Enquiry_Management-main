Who We Are

The Enquiry Management System is a web application developed using Django, Python, and Bootstrap. It enables users to submit enquiries online while providing administrators with an easy-to-use interface to manage and respond to them efficiently.

Our goal is to simplify communication between users and organizations by offering a fast, secure, and user-friendly enquiry process.
